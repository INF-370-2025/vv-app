 Help Center

Dashboard Help

Welcome to your Customer Dashboard! This page provides quick access to key features related to your jobs, quotes, and invoices.

Key Features Include

  1. Job Requests
    • View and manage your submitted job requests.
    • Track the status of ongoing work.
  2. Quotations
    • Review quotes sent by the company.
    • Approve or request changes to pricing and services.
  3. Invoices
    • Check issued invoices and payment status.
    • Download or print invoices for your records.
  4. Delivery Notes
    • Access proof of delivery for completed jobs.
    • Verify received services or products.
  5. Filtering Options: Stock levels can be filtered by type for better analysis

How to use:

  • Click any of the cards to navigate to the relevant section.
  • Contact support if you need help with quotes, payments, or job updates.

This dashboard helps you stay informed and manage your interactions with the company efficiently.
