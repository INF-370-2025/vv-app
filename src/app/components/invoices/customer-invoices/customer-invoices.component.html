Help Center

Invoices Help

This page allows you to view and manage your invoices, including tracking payment status and uploading proof of payment.

Key Features

1. Invoice Tabs

  • Unpaid Invoices – View outstanding payments and upload proof of payment.
  • Paid Invoices – See completed transactions and download receipts.

2. Search Function

Quickly find invoices by:

  • Invoice number
  • Job title
  • Customer name
  • Company name

3. Actions

  • View Invoice – Open a PDF copy of the invoice in a new tab.
  • Upload Proof of Payment – Submit payment confirmation (bank slip, receipt, etc.).

How to Use

Viewing an Invoice

  1. Click the button next to an invoice.
  2. The PDF will open in a new tab for download or printing.

Uploading Proof of Payment (For Unpaid Invoices)

  1. Click on an unpaid invoice.
  2. Attach your payment confirmation (PDF, image, or screenshot).
  3. Submit to notify the company.
