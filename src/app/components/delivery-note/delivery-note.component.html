Delivery Note Help

Key Features Include

  1. View Delivery Notes - Click the "View" button to open a note in a new tab.
  2. Delete Delivery Notes - Remove outdated or incorrect notes (requires confirmation).
  3. Create New Notes Click "Create Delivery Note" to generate a new document.

How to use:

  • View a Note → Click the eye icon next to a note to open it.
  • Delete a Note → Click the trash icon, then confirm deletion.
  • Add a New Note → Click the "Create Delivery Note" button and fill in the required details

⚠ Note: Once deleted, a delivery note cannot be recovered.
